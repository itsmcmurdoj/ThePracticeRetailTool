01 MDM & Automated iPad Fleet Management
▼
Strategic Value & Talking Points
  • •
    Risk Mitigation (Eliminating Personal Apple IDs): Transitioned all iPads and MacBooks from an unmanaged consumer Apple Account to an official Apple Business Manager (ABM) tenant. Completely eliminates the risk of permanent device Activation Lock if an employee leaves.
  • •
    Zero-Touch Scalability: Pre-configured Supervised MDM profiles. Any new iPad unboxes and configures itself over Wi-Fi, automatically installing *The Practice*, Wi-Fi certs, and kiosk locks with zero manual technician setup.
  • •
    Simplified 4-Tile Kiosk UI: Replaced the confusing default iOS icon grid with 4 massive, high-contrast action tiles. Slashes new employee tech onboarding from 2 hours to under 5 minutes.
02 Multi-Zone Acoustic Matrix & VSSL Soundtrack
▼
Strategic Value & Talking Points
  • •
    Sanctuary Sound Integrity: Unified multi-channel VSSL streaming amplifiers into discrete acoustic zones (Movement Studio, Boutique Retail, Cafe, and Private Hall). Prevents retail music from bleeding into quiet meditation classes.
  • •
    Teacher Fail-Safe Preset: Designed a dedicated 1-tap ambient restore button on the Yoga Studio iPad. If a live playlist hiccups, instructors can restore calming 432Hz soundscapes within 3 seconds without touching amp racks.
03 Digital Checkout & Automated Labeling (Moments / Nelko / Munbyn)
▼
Strategic Value & Talking Points
  • •
    Eliminating Queue Bottlenecks: Configured Moments POS into dedicated, locked kiosk profiles with rapid 1-tap checkout. Prevents app accidental closures and streamlines class rush-hour check-ins.
  • •
    Role-Isolated Thermal Printing: Standardized Nelko Bluetooth for instant retail shelf tags and cafe cup labeling; standardized Munbyn High-Speed Thermal for inventory receiving and shipping across 730+ active SKUs.
04 Proprietary Software: "The Practice" HTML Web App
▼
Strategic Value & Talking Points
  • •
    PWA Standalone Deployment: Pushed The Practice as a full-screen, native-feeling Web Clip with custom precomposed icons, removing Safari URL bars and navigation distractions.
  • •
    Offline Resilience & Vector Search: Maintained local vector embeddings (`product_embeddings.js`) and Service Worker caching, allowing front-of-house staff to look up holistic benefits and ingredients even during temporary internet hiccups.
05 Enterprise Network Security & IoT Segmentation
▼
Strategic Value & Talking Points
  • •
    VLAN Isolation for Payment Compliance: Separated store traffic into 3 discrete virtual networks: (1) Staff POS VLAN, (2) IoT Sanctuary (VSSL, Google Home, Printers), and (3) Client Guest Wi-Fi.
  • •
    Bandwidth QoS Prioritization: Guaranteed that high-traffic guest Wi-Fi usage never throttles Moments payment processing or VSSL high-res audio streaming.
